Meet Dr. Valencia

Doctor of Physical Therapy · ISSA-Certified Trainer · Lifestyle Fitness Coach · Certified Lymph Therapist

Growing up in Manhattan, Dr. Valencia discovered his passion for fitness coaching at 17, working in some of the city's most respected training environments. After choosing to serve his country — spending five years in the United States Navy — a personal injury during his service became a turning point. It deepened his understanding of recovery, resilience, and the real difference expert physical guidance makes.

"If you want something bad enough, you can achieve it — but you can't do it alone. Asking for help is a strength, not a weakness."

That belief shapes everything at Valencia Health and Wellness — a collaborative, non-judgmental approach that pairs clinical precision with motivational coaching.

Dr. Valencia bridges the gap between clinical rehabilitation and everyday fitness. Every client starts with a comprehensive evaluation — tailored, not templated.
