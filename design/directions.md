# Directions

Each is the home hero plus the first section below it, rendered with self-hosted Inter,
Space Mono and Playfair Display at 390 and 1280. All three carry the pulse line (user's
chosen signature moment). Side by side: `directions/compare.png`.

## A · Clinical pulse
- Primary reference: 589c0a33-a38c-4f77-a7b0-bf0c602c937d · Superpower (opened)
- Traits: full-bleed black-and-white photo of Bruce at work under a centred white Inter 700 headline; crimson only on the one primary pill and the pulse, which draws along the bottom edge of the hero; white contained sections after; services as full-width rows (borrowed from e887d8c2 TidyCal, seen); mono credential line.
- Rejects: card grids, icon tiles, serif display, a second accent, dark canvas below the hero.
- Shots: directions/shots/a/

## B · Quiet authority
- Primary reference: 0ab6a10a-bf24-4031-bf51-9a3f55dc8580 · Legora (opened)
- Traits: warm white canvas; Playfair Display 400 at up to 88px with -0.02em tracking (Legora's own hero face); black 2px-radius buttons with no brand colour; contained 8px-radius photo beside the headline (z-pattern); crimson appears only as the pulse, a hairline rule under the hero.
- Rejects: pill buttons, filled colour CTAs, full-bleed imagery competing with the headline.
- Risk: this is the calm-editorial serif pattern the anti-slop guide warns about; it is here because Legora supports it and the old site used Playfair, so you can see it next to the alternatives.
- Shots: directions/shots/b/

## C · Studio dark
- Primary reference: 1b7e4f5c-c3c2-48d5-8f34-3ecdd17f422e · Peloton (opened)
- Traits: charcoal `#181a1d` canvas end to end; centred white Inter headline; crimson only on 28px-radius CTAs and the pulse, which reads like a monitor trace on dark; wide 21:9 photo of Bruce under the hero; a four-up "lineup" of generated photos with 0px tiles and 6px image radius.
- Rejects: shadows, gradients, light sections, more than one accent.
- Closest to donovanhunter.com's darkness, which is also the main risk: dark by default reads colder for older and post-surgical clients.
- Shots: directions/shots/c/
